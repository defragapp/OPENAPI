# Sovereign.OS public visual contract

The public experience explains Sovereign.OS through a real product moment. The focal visual is an interactive Sovereign answer at readable product scale.

## Hero

- Kicker: `PRIVATE AI, BUILT AROUND YOUR BASELINE`
- Headline: `Know yourself. Understand the system. Choose what fits.`
- Primary action: `Build my Baseline`
- Secondary action: `See what Sovereign can answer`

The answer stage includes the sample leadership-and-responsibility question, a direct answer with specific mechanism, selectable Shadow, Gift, Alignment, and Basis sections, a practical experiment, and an explicitly labeled sanitized exact-data fixture.

It must feel like a finished app surface rather than a marketing mockup.

## Page sequence

1. Real Sovereign answer.
2. Horizontally selectable question rail updating one shared answer stage.
3. Stable Baseline plus expiring current overlay, user confirmation, and visible unknown.
4. One central quality with selectable Shadow, Gift, and Alignment behavior.
5. Permission journey and a two-person relationship view with a full-width interaction field.
6. A supported system map with readable people, roles, one active connection, a separate pressure field, and a quiet contextual Covenant action.
7. Exact Basis strip and data-only source detail.
8. Accurate Free and Sovereign+ pricing.
9. Final Baseline-first call to action.

## Visual system

Use the shared `--sov-*` tokens. Preserve near-black charcoal, warm paper, restrained clay, muted sage, quiet depth, generous space, readable editorial display type, and modern system UI type.

The page uses one focal point per scene. It avoids symbolic spiritual ornament, decorative charts, neon AI imagery, a therapy-dashboard look, and grids of interchangeable feature claims.

Primary containers use 20–28px radii. Compact controls use 10–14px radii or a deliberate pill. Borders remain quiet. Motion explains state or context arrival, respects reduced motion, and never prevents reading.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- Public headings do not exceed 68px desktop or 46px mobile.
- Body copy is 16–18px with a readable measure.
- All controls have visible focus and at least 44px targets.
- Tabs and disclosures use correct semantics and keyboard behavior.
- Exact glyphs have accessible labels.
- No essential information depends on hover.
- No horizontal page overflow occurs at 320px.
- The mobile Basis strip stays one line and reveals `+N` for hidden values.
- Source and context detail uses a bottom-sheet pattern on mobile.
