# Public Intelligence Demonstrations v2 — Acceptance Boundary

This document records the bounded acceptance contract for the three public Sovereign.OS demonstrations on the landing page.

## Scope

Only these public product demonstrations are in scope:

1. `01 · You`
2. `02 · You + your people`
3. `03 · From 1:1 to the whole system`

Out of scope: hero, public navigation, pricing, authentication, onboarding behavior, billing, consent behavior, Baseline computation, AI runtime, authenticated workspace architecture, and deployment topology.

## Experience hierarchy

Each demonstration is text-first:

1. ordinary question;
2. specific direct Sovereign answer;
3. one visual understanding moment;
4. one especially useful distinction;
5. one natural continuation;
6. collapsed `See source details`;
7. quiet `Does this fit?` calibration.

The public UI must not expose processing steps or chain-of-thought. Historical workflow strings may remain only as non-rendered release-adapter lineage fingerprints.

## Locked questions

- You: `How do I make decisions that actually fit me?`
- You + your people: `Why does the same conversation feel urgent to me and pressuring to them?`
- Whole system: `What changes when I stop playing the role everyone expects?`

## Visual progression

- Self: one person and the distinction between a choice and adapting the choice for outside reception.
- Relationship: two distinct people with the interaction itself centered as `What happens between you`.
- System: supplied participants and roles with the communication route shown before and after the user stops mediating.

The visual language may use lines, spacing, position, movement, connection, compression, divergence, and convergence. It must not present scores, measurements, compatibility, diagnostic claims, or technical geometry language.

## Relationship boundary

- Both people choose what they share.
- Each person remains distinct.
- No compatibility score.
- No motive, exact emotion, or private-thought claim.
- Only the participant can confirm what they actually felt or intended.

## System boundary

- Roles and events are supplied by the example, not inferred from Baseline data.
- Do not introduce authority as a Systems intelligence dimension.
- Do not introduce missing perspective as a Systems intelligence dimension.
- Do not assign motives or absent-person perspectives.
- Do not frame pressure movement as causation.

## Source treatment

Representative exact source values remain collapsed under `See source details`. The answer must remain complete if source details are never opened.

## Motion and accessibility

- One finite reveal sequence when the section enters the viewport.
- No infinite demo motion.
- No meaning depends on animation.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` exposes the complete static state.
- Calibration controls remain at least 44×44px.
- Mobile preserves the self, relationship, and system visuals rather than replacing them with text-only fallbacks.

## Human visual acceptance

Before merge or production release, capture desktop and iPhone screenshots of each demonstration without surrounding marketing copy. Each isolated screenshot must independently communicate:

1. Sovereign understands an individual through an existing personal reference.
2. Sovereign can understand what happens between two distinct people.
3. Sovereign can understand how several people create a larger recurring system.

A screenshot fails if it reads primarily as a generic chat conversation, personality test, workflow diagram, dashboard, SaaS feature card, astrology product, or counseling worksheet.
