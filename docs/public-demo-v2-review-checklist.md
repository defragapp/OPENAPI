# Public Demo v2 — Human Review Checklist

Review the three landing demonstrations independently at desktop and iPhone widths before merge.

## 01 · You

- The question is immediately readable.
- The direct Sovereign answer dominates before any source detail.
- The personal visual makes the distinction between the choice and making the choice acceptable understandable without technical labels.
- The demo communicates breadth of personal intelligence rather than defining self exploration through conflict or distress.
- No score, meter, assessment, or framework code is visible by default.

## 02 · You + your people

- You and Alex remain visually distinct.
- `What happens between you` is the visual center.
- The interaction loop is understandable without blame or causation language.
- The timing bridge is visible without becoming a counseling worksheet.
- The permission boundary is quiet but readable.
- No compatibility score or private-state claim appears.

## 03 · From 1:1 to the whole system

- Parent, You, and Sibling remain distinct.
- The supplied scenario is clearly separated from Baseline interpretation.
- The current communication route through You is immediately understandable.
- The changed state keeps You in the family while removing the mediation route.
- The viewer can see that changing one position forces the structure to reorganize.
- No inferred role, absent-person perspective, authority dimension, or causal claim appears.

## Shared system

- One mature product visual language across all three.
- Native enterprise sans typography only.
- Near-black / cream / warm-metal palette.
- No browser traffic-light chrome.
- No five-step workflow panel.
- No dashboard/card-grid feeling.
- No infinite demo motion.
- Source details collapsed.
- `Does this fit?` remains quiet and at least 44px touchable.
- Mobile retains the actual visualization.
- No horizontal overflow at 375, 390, or 430px.
