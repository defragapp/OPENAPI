# Public Demo v2 — Motion Boundary

Motion is explanatory only.

Each demonstration may reveal once when entering the viewport:
1. question;
2. direct answer;
3. comprehension visual;
4. key distinction.

The animation stops after that sequence.

No public demonstration may use infinite movement, floating nodes, spinning fields, decorative particles, or motion required to reveal meaning.

Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, every question, answer, visual, distinction, continuation, calibration control, and source disclosure must be present in its complete static state.
