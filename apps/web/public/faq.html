THE PRODUCT

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a private AI for understanding yourself, a decision, a relationship, or a group. It starts with your Baseline, then adds temporary current context or information from other people only when you choose and the required permission exists.

How is Sovereign different from a general AI assistant?

A general AI usually starts with the current prompt. Sovereign starts with your private Baseline, so it has a consistent reference for personal questions across different conversations.

What is a Baseline?

Your Baseline is the private personal starting point Sovereign uses when answering questions about how you may decide, communicate, learn, connect, lead, create, and respond under pressure. It is interpretive and open to correction.

What can I ask?

Ask about yourself, a decision, communication, pressure, love, leadership, boundaries, Shadow and Gift, a relationship, a family role, a team problem, or any part of your Baseline you want explained more clearly.

Where does the Baseline come from?

Baseline Design uses calculated astronomical positions and selected interpretive frameworks, including astrology, partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations, and numerology. Sovereign.OS is not a horoscope, prediction product, or scientific personality test. It turns those sources into possibilities you can examine, correct, or reject.

Is this therapy?

No. Sovereign.OS does not diagnose, provide treatment, or replace professional care. It helps you examine personal, relationship, and group context in plain language while keeping your judgment primary.

Can Sovereign tell me what another person feels or intends?

No. With permission, Sovereign can compare the Baseline information each person agreed to share and explain the interaction. It cannot know another person’s exact feelings, motives, private experience, or future behavior.

How do invitations work?

You send an invitation. The other person connects their own account and chooses each permission separately. You cannot approve access for them, and they can change or revoke permission later.

What is Covenant?

Covenant is an optional Christian Scripture perspective for a relevant personal, relationship, or family question. You must choose it before it appears, and it stays separate from the main Baseline answer.

Does Sovereign make decisions for me?

No. Sovereign can show what supports a choice, what works against it, the tradeoff involved, and what information is still missing. The decision remains yours.

PLANS AND PRIVACY

What does Free include?

Free includes your complete private Baseline, Today, optional temporary current context, personal exploration, corrections, and 10 Sovereign AI turns each month. No card is required.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly AI turns, permission-based relationship comparisons, family and team views, saved insights in Library, and optional Covenant. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

What reaches the language model?

Sovereign sends only the reduced context needed for the current question. Raw birth details, exact private location, secrets, and unrelated account history are excluded.

Can I correct or remove an interpretation?

Yes. You can mark what fits, partly fits, or does not fit; decide what enters Library; revoke shared permissions; and request account deletion.
