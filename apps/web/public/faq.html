FAQ

Clear answers before you begin.

Sovereign is a private decision-intelligence workspace. It uses your Baseline, current context, your corrections, and only the information you choose to save or share.

What is a Baseline?

A Baseline is a private starting point for how you tend to decide, communicate, learn, connect, and respond to pressure. It gives Sovereign context without turning a tendency into a fixed identity.

What does Sovereign actually help me do?

It separates enduring tendencies, current pressure, what was actually observed, and what remains unknown. That structure helps you leave with clearer words, timing, a boundary, a repair, or a deliberate pause.

Does Sovereign tell me what is true about another person?

No. It separates what you observed, what another person consented to share, what may be influencing the interaction, and what remains unknown. It does not claim hidden motives or exact inner states.

Is the visual archetype the source of an answer?

No. When a visual archetype appears, the grounded answer has already been formed from confirmed experience and permitted context. The rendered component helps explain a role or movement; it does not create the conclusion.

What happens to my birth information?

Raw birth input stays inside the private computation boundary. The AI receives reduced, relevant results rather than the raw details or exact private location.

Can I include another person?

Only through identity-bound, scope-specific permission. The other person reviews each requested use and can deny or revoke it later.

What is saved?

Nothing becomes a lasting Library item unless you choose to save it. Unsaved thread content is scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Minimal non-content security records may remain for up to 90 days.

What does Free include?

Free is a permanent plan with Baseline setup, Today, Explore, and 10 Sovereign AI turns per UTC calendar month. No card is required.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ includes 300 Sovereign AI turns per UTC calendar month and adds consented People comparisons, family and team Systems, Library continuity, and the optional Covenant lens. It is $20 monthly or $99 annually.

Can I export or share my workspace?

Private account export is not available at launch. You can share the public Sovereign.OS site, and invited-person features use explicit permission. Public sharing never includes private workspace data.

What happens if I cancel Sovereign+?

Your paid features resolve safely to Free. Cancellation does not delete your workspace. You can manage the subscription and payment method through Stripe’s billing portal.

Is Covenant always included in an answer?

No. Covenant is an optional biblical lens that must be deliberately enabled. It never automatically requires contact, estrangement, reconciliation, forgiveness, submission, or continued exposure to harm.

Is Sovereign therapy or a diagnosis?

No. Sovereign is non-diagnostic software for understanding context and making clearer choices. Use qualified professional support for medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or urgent safety needs.
