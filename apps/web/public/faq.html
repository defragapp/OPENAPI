QUESTIONS

What you should know before you begin.

What Sovereign does, what stays private, how relationships work with permission, and what changes between Free and Sovereign+.

THE PRODUCT

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a private personal AI built around your Baseline Design. It helps you understand recurring patterns, decisions, relationships, and groups without making you explain your whole context again every time.

What is Baseline Design?

Baseline Design is the private personal foundation Sovereign uses to understand where to begin. It gives the product a consistent reference for how you may decide, communicate, learn, connect, lead, create, respond under pressure, and grow.

How is Sovereign different from a general AI assistant?

A general assistant usually begins with the current conversation. Sovereign can begin from your Baseline, then bring in current, relationship, or system context only when it belongs to the question.

What can I use Sovereign to understand?

Recurring patterns, decisions, pressure points, relationships, family roles, team dynamics, Shadow and Gift, Alignment, temporary current context, or any part of your Baseline you want to understand more clearly.

What are Shadow, Gift, and Alignment?

Shadow describes how a valid quality may narrow, protect, avoid, or overreach. Gift describes what that same quality can make possible when used with awareness. Alignment helps you examine what supports a choice, what pulls against it, and the tradeoff that remains. None is a score.

Does Sovereign make decisions for me?

No. It can make a pattern or tradeoff easier to see. The decision and final judgment remain yours.

PEOPLE + PERMISSION

How do relationships work?

Another person connects their own account and chooses what they want to share. Sovereign keeps each person distinct and looks at what happens between you without pretending to know private motives or feelings.

Can I add someone’s Baseline without them agreeing?

No. A name or birth detail does not create permission. The other person connects their own account and controls what can be used.

Can Sovereign tell me what another person feels?

No. It can describe differences and interaction patterns using information both people have agreed to share, but it cannot know someone else’s exact feelings, motives, private experience, or future behavior.

What happens in a System?

Sovereign helps you see how roles, authority, responsibility, dependence, pressure, and different perspectives shape a family, household, friendship group, workplace, or team. It does not assume everyone carries the same responsibility.

Can permission be changed later?

Yes. Each person can change or revoke their own sharing choices. New relationship and system use follows the current permission choices.

FRAMEWORKS + LIMITS

Is this astrology?

Astrology is one source inside Baseline Design, but Sovereign.OS is not a horoscope or prediction product. Baseline Design also uses partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations and numerology, then translates those sources into patterns you can examine and correct.

Which frameworks are included?

The launch Baseline uses calculated astronomical positions, astrology, partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations, and numerology. Tarot is not part of Sovereign.OS. These frameworks support reflection; they are not scientific personality measurements, diagnosis, or proof of future outcomes.

Is this therapy?

No. Sovereign.OS does not diagnose, provide treatment, or replace professional care. It is designed to help you understand personal, relationship, and system context in clear language while keeping your judgment primary.

Can Sovereign prove a diagnosis, hidden motive, spiritual cause, or future outcome?

No. A framework, coincidence, current astronomical condition, or strong feeling is not treated as proof of a diagnosis, hidden motive, spiritual cause, or future outcome.

What is Basis?

Sovereign answers in plain language first. If you want to look underneath an answer, Basis shows the exact source values that informed it.

What does “what keeps the pattern going” mean?

It does not mean everyone is to blame. Sovereign can look at roles, pressure, responsibility, boundaries, and what may be changeable without turning understanding into shared blame.

PRIVACY + ACCOUNT

What reaches the language model?

Sovereign sends only the context needed to answer the question. Raw birth details, exact private location, authentication material, payment identifiers, invitation tokens, and unrelated account history stay out of the model context.

Can I correct or remove an interpretation?

Yes. You can confirm what fits, mark what partly fits, reject what does not fit, control what enters Library, revoke sharing permission, download your account data, and request account deletion.

Do I need to open my email every time I sign in?

No. After the account is verified, you can add a passkey and use a supported device authentication method such as Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your device lock. Email remains available for verification and recovery.

What happens to my plan if a payment fails or I cancel?

Free remains available without a card. Sovereign+ stays active while the paid subscription is active. If paid access ends, your account stays open and returns to Free.

Can I report an accuracy, privacy, or safety problem?

Yes. Use the correction controls beneath an ordinary answer or contact info@defrag.app. Do not include passwords, API keys, exact private location, or another person’s private information.

PLANS + SUPPORT

What does Free include?

Free includes the complete private Baseline, Today and temporary current context, personal exploration, fit corrections, and 10 Sovereign AI turns each month. It is permanent and requires no card.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly turns, permission-based relationship comparisons, family and team Systems, Library continuity, and contextual Covenant exploration. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

What is Covenant?

Covenant is an optional Sovereign+ lens for exploring a relevant personal, relationship, or family situation through Christian teaching and clearly cited Scripture. It appears only when you choose it and does not replace the ordinary Sovereign answer.

Can I support Sovereign.OS without subscribing?

Yes. Support Sovereign.OS with any one-time amount from $1. Support is separate from Free and Sovereign+, is not presented as tax-deductible, and does not unlock paid features or change your plan.

SAFETY

What happens when a message may indicate immediate danger?

Sovereign pauses ordinary interpretation and gives a direct, human-support-first response. It does not turn that moment into an upgrade prompt.

Does safety depend on my plan?

No. Safety responses are available on every plan and do not use one of your monthly Sovereign AI turns.
