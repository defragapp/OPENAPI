THE PLATFORM

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a personal and relational intelligence platform powered by Baseline Design. It helps you understand yourself, explore shadow and light, examine alignment, understand relationships from both sides, and map how families, teams, and other human systems function.

What would I use it for?

Explore your own design, understand a difficult part of yourself, examine a decision or behavior, consider whether a relationship fits who you are, compare two permitted Baseline Designs, map a family or team, or add an optional Christian and biblical perspective through Covenant.

Do I have to arrive with a problem?

No. Your Baseline Design is useful on its own. You can explore identity, qualities, strengths, shadow and light, communication, relationships, choices, pressure, purpose, and development without beginning with an incident.

How is this different from a normal AI chat?

A normal chat begins with whatever you type into an empty box. Sovereign works from your Baseline Design and the specific personal, relational, system, or Covenant context you intentionally choose.

Does Sovereign tell me what to do?

No. It can help you understand a choice, identify tradeoffs, and explore what alignment may look like. Your judgment remains final, and you can correct or reject anything that does not fit your experience.

BASELINE DESIGN

What is Baseline Design?

Baseline Design is the personal intelligence framework at the center of Sovereign.OS. It organizes the platform’s natal-data framework into an explorable understanding of archetypal qualities, roles, strengths, tensions, communication, decisions, relationships, and responses under pressure.

What information builds it?

You provide a birth date, birthplace and timezone, birth-time certainty, and a birth time when known. The calculation uses astronomical positions and selected symbolic frameworks, then translates the result into ordinary language.

What do shadow and light mean?

They describe different expressions of the same underlying quality. A quality may become protective, reactive, suppressed, exaggerated, integrated, or deeply useful depending on how it is being lived. Shadow is not a bad identity, and light is not perfection.

What does alignment mean?

Alignment is the question of whether a decision, behavior, relationship, environment, or direction supports your deeper design. Sovereign.OS helps you examine the fit; it does not make the decision for you.

Is it a scientific personality test?

No. Astrology, numerology, and the partial Human Design and Gene Keys signals used by the product are symbolic interpretive frameworks. They are reflection material—not diagnosis, psychological fact, proof of current behavior, or prediction.

Do I need to understand those frameworks?

No. You use Sovereign in ordinary language. Supporting source details are secondary and optional.

What if something does not fit?

Mark it as fitting, partly fitting, or not true today. Your experience remains authoritative, and a correction does not rewrite your entire Baseline as if one response changed who you are.

RELATIONSHIPS AND SYSTEMS

How does a two-person view work?

You can add someone privately, then invite them. They connect their own account and decide which uses to allow. Only after permission is active can Sovereign compare the relevant context from both people.

What can a relationship comparison explore?

It can explore different needs, communication styles, roles, tension, connection, boundaries, strengths, defenses, accountability, and mutual influence. It is not a compatibility score and does not decide who is right.

What does a System add?

A System brings multiple people and the wider structure into view: family roles, caregiving, authority, responsibility, dependence, loyalties, recurring expectations, constraints, and how one person’s change affects the group.

Can Sovereign know why another person did something?

No. It can explore possibilities using permitted Baseline context, observed behavior, roles, authority, dependence, and practical constraints. It cannot establish another person’s private motive, exact feeling, diagnosis, or future choice.

Does understanding the other side excuse harm?

No. Understanding, agreement, forgiveness, reconciliation, trust, and continued contact are different decisions. Perspective can increase clarity without removing accountability or boundaries.

COVENANT

What is Covenant?

Covenant is an optional Christian and biblical lens. When you enable it, Sovereign can connect personal and relational questions with clearly cited scripture and Christian teachings about responsibility, truth, compassion, forgiveness, reconciliation, boundaries, accountability, humility, discernment, and repair.

Does Covenant claim to know God’s exact intent?

No. Scripture remains distinct from Baseline Design interpretation. Covenant must not claim divine certainty, speak on behalf of God, or use scripture to require contact, estrangement, reconciliation, forgiveness, submission, or continued exposure to harm.

PRIVACY AND CONTINUITY

What reaches the language model?

Sovereign receives the reduced, plain-language context needed for a question. Raw birth inputs, exact private location, and unrelated account history are excluded.

What does Library save?

Only an understanding you deliberately choose to keep becomes a Library item. It returns with the context that made it useful. Unsaved conversations do not become a permanent personal archive.

Can I share my private account publicly?

No. Public sharing sends the Sovereign.OS product link only. Private account export is not available at launch.

PLANS AND SUPPORT

What does Free include?

Free is an ongoing plan with the complete Baseline, Today, Explore, fit corrections, and 10 Sovereign responses per UTC calendar month. No card is required.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly responses, permission-based two-person comparisons, family and team Systems, saved Library continuity, and the optional Covenant lens. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

Can I support development without changing my plan?

Yes. The optional Stripe development-support link accepts a one-time amount from $5 to $500. A contribution does not purchase Free or Sovereign+ access, create an entitlement, grant ownership or influence, or promise a future feature. Sovereign.OS does not represent contributions as tax-deductible. Payment questions and refund requests can be sent to info@defrag.app.

Is Sovereign therapy or professional advice?

No. Sovereign.OS is reflection and decision-support software. It does not replace mental-health, medical, legal, financial, emergency, pastoral, or other qualified professional support.
