THE PRODUCT

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a private personal and relational intelligence platform. It helps you understand yourself, make clearer decisions, and navigate relationships, families, and teams using context you choose to provide.

How is Sovereign different from general AI?

Most AI starts from the current prompt. Sovereign carries forward a private personal foundation, so future answers can reflect how you decide, communicate, relate, and respond under pressure.

What is the personal foundation?

It is the private personal context Sovereign carries into future questions. Inside the product, this foundation is called your Baseline. It is interpretive, open to correction, and not a scientific measurement of personality.

What can Sovereign help me understand?

Explore yourself, a decision, communication, pressure, love, leadership, boundaries, a relationship, a family role, a team challenge, or another situation where personal and relational context matters.

How is the personal foundation calculated?

Baseline Design uses calculated astronomical positions and selected interpretive frameworks, including astrology, partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations, and numerology. These sources support reflection. They are not prediction, diagnosis, or scientific personality measurement.

Is this therapy?

No. Sovereign.OS does not diagnose, provide treatment, or replace professional care. It helps you examine personal, relationship, and group context while keeping your judgment central.

Can Sovereign know another person’s private experience?

No. With permission, Sovereign can compare the personal context each person approved and explain how their different needs or approaches may interact. The other person remains the authority on their own feelings, motives, and experience.

How do invitations work?

You send an invitation. The other person connects their own account and reviews each requested permission separately. They can change or revoke those choices later.

What is the Christian Scripture perspective?

It is an optional way to add Christian teaching and cited Scripture to a relevant personal, relationship, or family question. Inside Sovereign, this capability is called Covenant. It appears only after you choose it and remains separate from the main answer.

Does Sovereign make decisions for me?

No. Sovereign can show what supports a choice, what makes it difficult, the tradeoff involved, and what information is still missing. The decision remains yours.

PLANS AND PRIVACY

What does Free include?

Free includes your complete private personal foundation, optional temporary current context, personal exploration, corrections, and 10 Sovereign AI turns each month. No card is required.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly AI turns, permission-based relationship comparisons, family and team intelligence, saved insights, and the optional Christian Scripture perspective. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

What reaches the language model?

Sovereign sends only the reduced context needed for the current question. Raw birth details, exact private location, secrets, and unrelated account history are excluded.

Can I correct or remove an interpretation?

Yes. You can mark what is useful, partly true, or not true for you; decide what is saved; revoke shared permissions; and request account deletion.
