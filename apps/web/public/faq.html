THE PRODUCT

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a private intelligence platform for understanding yourself, your relationships, and the groups you live and work inside. It gives one agent—Sovereign—a Baseline for you, then applies the relevant present, relational, or system context to a real question.

What would I use it for?

Use it to make a difficult choice, understand an unexpected reaction, prepare for a conversation, see why recurring friction continues, or clarify roles and responsibility in a family, household, friendship, workplace, or team.

How is this different from a normal AI chat?

A normal chat usually begins with whatever you type now. Sovereign begins with a private Baseline, keeps temporary pressure separate from steadier patterns, and can add permission-based relationship or group context when the question needs it.

Does Sovereign tell me what to do?

It can suggest one grounded next move, but it should also show the distinction behind that suggestion and leave room for you to correct or reject it. Your judgment remains final.

BASELINE DESIGN

What is a Baseline?

A Baseline is an interpretive starting map for decisions, communication, connection, learning, expression, and pressure response. It gives Sovereign consistent personal context before you describe a problem.

What information builds it?

You provide a birth date, birthplace and timezone, birth-time certainty, and a birth time when known. The calculation uses astronomical positions and selected symbolic frameworks, then translates the result into ordinary language.

Is it a scientific personality test?

No. Astrology, numerology, and the partial Human Design and Gene Keys signals used by the product are symbolic interpretive frameworks. They are reflection material—not diagnosis, psychological fact, proof of current behavior, or prediction.

Do I need to understand those frameworks?

No. You use Sovereign in ordinary language. Supporting source details are secondary and optional.

What if an answer does not fit?

Mark it as fitting, partly fitting, or not true today. That correction grounds the current conversation without rewriting your Baseline as if one moment changed who you are.

PEOPLE AND SYSTEMS

Can Sovereign know why another person did something?

No. It can compare permitted patterns, observed behavior, roles, authority, dependence, and practical constraints. It cannot establish another person’s private motive, exact feeling, diagnosis, or future choice.

How does a two-person view work?

You can add someone privately, then invite them. They connect their own account and decide which uses to allow. Only after permission is active can Sovereign compare the relevant context from both people.

What does a System add?

A System adds the structure a one-to-one comparison can miss: formal and informal roles, decision authority, responsibility, dependence, constraints, and shared goals across a family, household, friendship, workplace, or team.

PRIVACY AND CONTINUITY

What reaches the language model?

Sovereign receives the reduced, plain-language context needed for a question. Raw birth inputs, exact private location, and unrelated account history are excluded.

What does Library save?

Only an insight you deliberately choose to keep becomes a Library item. It returns with the context that made it useful. Unsaved conversations do not become a permanent personal archive.

Can I share my private workspace publicly?

No. Public sharing sends the Sovereign.OS product link only. Private account export is not available at launch.

PLANS AND LIMITS

What does Free include?

Free is an ongoing plan with the complete Baseline, Today, Explore, fit corrections, and 10 Sovereign responses per UTC calendar month. No card is required.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly responses, permission-based two-person comparisons, family and team Systems, saved Library continuity, and the optional Covenant lens. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

What is Covenant?

Covenant is an optional biblical lens that stays off until you enable it for a question or conversation. The grounded Sovereign answer remains complete without it.

Is Sovereign therapy or professional advice?

No. Sovereign.OS is reflection and decision-support software. It does not replace mental-health, medical, legal, financial, emergency, or other qualified professional support.
