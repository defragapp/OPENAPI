THE PRODUCT

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a private AI platform for understanding yourself, your relationships, and the groups around you. It starts with your Baseline Design, then adds only the current, relational, and system context you choose.

How is Sovereign different from a general AI assistant?

Most AI begins with the prompt in front of it. Sovereign begins with a private Baseline that gives it a consistent place to start, then uses only the additional context that belongs to the question.

What is Baseline Design?

Baseline Design is the personal intelligence foundation inside Sovereign.OS. It gives Sovereign a consistent reference for how you may decide, communicate, learn, connect, lead, create, respond under pressure, and grow.

What can I ask?

Ask about who you are, what may be more relevant now, Shadow and Gift, a decision, a behavior, a relationship, a family role, a team dynamic, or any part of your Baseline you want to understand more clearly.

Is this astrology?

Baseline Design uses calculated astronomical positions and selected interpretive frameworks, including astrology, partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations, and numerology. Sovereign.OS is not a horoscope, prediction product, or scientific personality test. It translates relevant source data into possibilities you can examine and correct.

Is this therapy?

No. Sovereign.OS does not diagnose, provide treatment, or replace professional care. It helps you explore personal, relational, and system context in clear language while keeping your judgment primary.

Can Sovereign tell me what another person feels?

No. With permission, Sovereign can show how two Baselines may interact. It cannot know another person’s exact feelings, motives, private experience, or future behavior.

How do invitations work?

You can invite another person into a relationship or system view. They connect their own account and choose what Sovereign may use. You cannot grant permission for them.

What is Covenant?

Covenant is an optional way to explore a relevant personal, relationship, or family question through Christian teaching and clearly cited Scripture. It appears only as an option and remains visibly separate from the grounded Baseline answer.

Does Sovereign make decisions for me?

No. Sovereign can show what supports a choice, what pulls against it, what the tradeoff may be, and what information is missing. The decision remains yours.

PLANS, SUPPORT, AND PRIVACY

What does Free include?

Free includes the complete private Baseline, Today and current-condition context, personal exploration, fit corrections, and 10 Sovereign AI turns per month. No card is required.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly turns, permission-based relationship comparisons, family and team Systems, Library continuity, and contextual Covenant exploration. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

Can I support Sovereign.OS without subscribing?

Yes. Sovereign.OS has two voluntary one-time support options: general support and continued development support. Contributions are separate from Free and Sovereign+, are not presented as tax-deductible, and do not grant access, entitlements, ownership, influence, or a promise of future features.

What reaches the language model?

Sovereign receives only the reduced context needed for the question. Raw birth input, exact private location, secrets, and unrelated account history remain excluded.

Can I correct or remove an interpretation?

Yes. You can confirm what fits, mark what only partly fits, reject what does not fit today, control what enters Library, revoke shared-context permission, and request account deletion.
