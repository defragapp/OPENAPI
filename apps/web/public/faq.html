QUESTIONS

What you should know before you begin.

What Sovereign does, what it does not claim, what stays private, how other people enter with permission, and what changes between Free and Sovereign+.

THE PRODUCT

What is Sovereign.OS?

Sovereign.OS is a private personal AI built around your Baseline Design. It helps you examine patterns, decisions, relationships, and groups while keeping interpretation, evidence, permission, and what is still unknown distinct.

What is Baseline Design?

Baseline Design is the private personal reference Sovereign uses across the product. It gives the system a consistent starting point for how you may decide, communicate, learn, connect, lead, create, respond under pressure, and grow.

How is Sovereign different from a general AI assistant?

A general assistant usually begins with the current conversation. Sovereign can begin from your validated Baseline, then add only the current, relationship, or system context that belongs to the situation.

What can I use Sovereign to examine?

Recurring patterns, decisions, pressure points, relationships, family roles, team dynamics, Shadow and Gift, Alignment, temporary current context, or any part of your Baseline you want to understand more clearly.

What are Shadow, Gift, and Alignment?

Shadow describes how a valid quality may narrow, protect, avoid, or overreach. Gift describes what that same quality may make possible when used with awareness. Alignment examines what supports a choice, what pulls against it, the tradeoff that remains, and what information is still needed. None is a score.

Does Sovereign make decisions for me?

No. It can make a pattern or tradeoff easier to see. The decision, correction, and final judgment remain yours.

PEOPLE + PERMISSION

How do relationships work?

Another person connects their own account and chooses what Sovereign may use. When permission exists, Sovereign keeps each person distinct and examines the interaction without claiming private motives or feelings.

Can I add someone’s Baseline without them agreeing?

No. A name or birth detail does not create permission. Relationship and system use is tied to the other person’s account and current consent choices.

Can Sovereign tell me what another person feels?

No. It can describe supported differences or interaction patterns using permitted context. It cannot know another person’s exact feelings, motives, private experience, or future behavior.

What happens in a System?

Sovereign can keep supplied roles, authority, responsibility, reliance, pressure, observations, and missing perspectives in view across a family, household, friendship group, workplace, or team. It does not assume that everyone contributed equally.

Can permission be changed later?

Yes. Each person can change or revoke their own sharing choices. New relationship and system use must respect the current permission state.

FRAMEWORKS + LIMITS

Is this astrology?

Astrology is one source used inside Baseline Design, but Sovereign.OS is not a horoscope or prediction product. Baseline Design also uses partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations and numerology, then translates relevant source data into possibilities you can examine and correct.

Which frameworks are included?

The launch Baseline uses calculated astronomical positions, astrology, partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations, and numerology. Tarot is not part of Sovereign.OS. These frameworks support reflection; they are not scientific personality measurements, diagnosis, or proof of future outcomes.

Is this therapy?

No. Sovereign.OS does not diagnose, provide treatment, or replace professional care. It is designed to help you examine personal, relationship, and system context in clear language while keeping your judgment primary.

Can Sovereign prove a diagnosis, hidden motive, spiritual cause, or future outcome?

No. It keeps what was supplied or observed separate from interpretation and what remains unknown. A framework, coincidence, current astronomical condition, or strong feeling is not treated as proof.

How does evidence appear?

The plain-language answer comes first. Exact server-approved Basis values can appear beneath it so you can inspect what supported the interpretation without turning the answer into a technical report.

What does “what keeps the pattern going” mean?

It does not mean everyone is to blame. Contribution is not causation, feedback is not blame, and understanding an interaction does not make harm mutual. Sovereign keeps responsibility and supported context distinct.

PRIVACY + ACCOUNT

What reaches the language model?

Only reduced context needed for the situation is sent. Raw birth input, exact private location, authentication material, payment identifiers, invitation tokens, and unrelated account history stay out of model context.

Can I correct or remove an interpretation?

Yes. You can confirm what fits, mark what partly fits, reject what does not fit, control what enters Library, revoke shared-context permission, download your account data, and request account deletion.

Do I need to open my email every time I sign in?

No. After the account is verified, you can add a passkey and use a supported device authentication method such as Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your device lock. Email remains available for verification and recovery.

When is my plan verified?

After secure sign-in, Sovereign.OS verifies the account’s current plan before opening the private workspace. Free remains Free without a payment record. Sovereign+ is enabled only from the server-confirmed Stripe subscription state.

Can I report an accuracy, privacy, or safety problem?

Yes. Use the correction controls beneath an ordinary answer or contact info@defrag.app. Do not include passwords, API keys, exact private location, or another person’s private information.

PLANS + SUPPORT

What does Free include?

Free includes the complete private Baseline, Today and temporary current context, personal exploration, fit corrections, and 10 Sovereign AI turns each month. It is permanent and requires no card.

What does Sovereign+ include?

Sovereign+ adds 300 monthly turns, permission-based relationship comparisons, family and team Systems, Library continuity, and contextual Covenant exploration. It costs $20 monthly or $99 annually.

What is Covenant?

Covenant is an optional Sovereign+ lens for exploring a relevant personal, relationship, or family situation through Christian teaching and clearly cited Scripture. It appears only when intentionally selected and remains separate from the grounded Baseline answer.

Can I support Sovereign.OS without subscribing?

Yes. Sovereign.OS has two voluntary one-time support options: general support and continued development support. Contributions are separate from Free and Sovereign+, are not presented as tax-deductible, and do not grant access, entitlements, ownership, influence, or a promise of future features.

SAFETY

What happens when a message may indicate immediate danger?

Sovereign pauses ordinary interpretation and presents a direct, human-support-first response. Safety handling does not become an upgrade prompt and does not use relationship or system context to intensify the response.

Does safety depend on my plan?

No. Grounded and urgent safety handling is available before plan allowances are checked and does not consume a monthly Sovereign AI turn.
