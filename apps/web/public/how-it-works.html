SELF EXPLORATION

See how a personal question becomes specific.

Sovereign answers in plain language first. The deeper detail stays underneath so the conversation remains easy to follow.

Sovereign · YouREPRESENTATIVE EXAMPLE

How do I know whether I’m refining an idea because it is getting clearer—or changing it because I’m anticipating everyone else’s reaction?

A useful distinction may be whether the next change makes the idea more coherent to you or merely more defensible to an imagined audience. Under pressure, sensitivity to meaning and response can become editing before you have decided what you actually want to preserve.
