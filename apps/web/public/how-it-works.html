HOW SOVEREIGN WORKS

Start with you. Add context when it matters.

Sovereign begins with your Baseline—a private reference for how you may think, decide, communicate, create, connect, and respond. It adds current, relationship, or system context only when it belongs to the question.

YOU → PEOPLE → SYSTEMS

Explore yourself first. Expand outward when you need to.

Use Sovereign for self-exploration, bring in another person with permission, or step back to see a family, team, household, or wider system.

01

Explore yourself.

Look at how you decide, communicate, create, connect, respond under pressure, and recognize what feels aligned.

02

Add what is happening now.

Current conditions can make part of your Baseline more relevant for a limited time without defining who you are.

03

Bring in your people with permission.

Relationship views keep each person distinct so differences and interaction patterns do not become mind-reading.

04

See the wider system.

When a family, team, household, or group is involved, keep consented people, supplied roles, permitted perspectives, confirmed responsibilities, reliance and care, pressure, constraints, observations, and interactions in view.

05

Answer first. Detail underneath.

Sovereign leads with the useful answer. Basis and correction controls are there when you want to inspect or challenge it.

THE CONVERSATION STAYS PRIMARY

Get the answer. Go deeper when you want.

Sovereign does not make you navigate a report before you get an answer. The answer comes first, deeper detail stays quiet beneath it, and the same thread is ready for the next question.

Where Baseline Design comes from

Baseline Design combines calculated astronomical data with selected interpretive frameworks. These sources support reflection; they are not scientific personality measurements, diagnosis, or prediction. The FAQ lists the included frameworks if you want the source detail.
