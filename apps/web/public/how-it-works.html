How it works

It begins before the conversation.

Sovereign.OS is useful before you explain a problem. Your Baseline Design, current conditions, consented relationships, system roles, and saved understanding form a private living context. Sovereign reads that context and brings forward what may matter now.

The intelligence flow

A large private context becomes one clear view.

The product does not show every calculation at once. It reduces the available context to the smallest useful explanation, keeps uncertainty visible, and lets you correct what does not fit.

Relationships

Permission comes before comparison.

A person is not a second chart added to your account. They are an identity with their own Baseline, choices, consent, and right to revoke access. Sovereign rechecks permission whenever shared context is used.

Invite

The person receives a private, time-limited invitation.

Choose

They decide each permission separately.

Use

Only the reduced context required for the selected view is available.

Revoke

Future use stops immediately when permission is withdrawn.

Continuity by choice

Save the understanding—not every private word.

Sovereign can offer a compact reflection when something becomes useful enough to keep. Nothing enters the Library unless you approve it.

Unsaved

Conversation context expires.

  • Unsaved thread content follows the 30-day retention policy.
  • Operational metadata remains separate from conversation content.
  • You can leave a thought without turning it into a permanent profile.
Saved

Your Library keeps what you chose.

  • A recognition you want to remember.
  • A clearer way to respond next time.
  • A short audio, card, visual, or learning module when available.
