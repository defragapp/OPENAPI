How Sovereign works

Personal AI needs more than your latest message.

Sovereign.OS starts with a private Baseline: a simple picture of how you tend to make decisions, communicate, learn, connect, and respond to pressure. It can then add what is current, the people you choose to include, and what you decide to save.

People stay in control

Sharing is a choice, not a shortcut.

Another person is never added as information you own. They receive a private invitation, choose what may be shared, and can change that choice later. Sovereign checks permission each time shared information is used.

Invite

The person receives a private, time-limited invitation.

Choose

They decide each permission separately.

Use

Only the information needed for that view is included.

Change

Future use stops when permission is removed.

Private by default

Keep control of what is used, shared, and saved.

YOUR DETAILS

Used to build your Baseline.

The AI receives reduced, relevant context—not the raw private details used to calculate it.

OTHER PEOPLE

Included only with permission.

Each person chooses what may be shared and can remove access later.

YOUR LIBRARY

Saved only when you choose.

Conversation history can expire while the short understanding you selected remains available.
