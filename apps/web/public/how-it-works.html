HOW IT WORKS

Create your personal foundation once. Use it whenever you need clarity.

Sovereign keeps the personal context you choose to create, then brings it into questions about yourself, a decision, a relationship, or a group. Shared information is added only with permission.

FIVE SIMPLE STEPS

Start with your context. Add only what the question needs.

The answer comes first. What is personal, temporary, confirmed, interpreted, or still uncertain remains clearly separated.

01

Create your personal foundation.

Add the birth details used to calculate your private starting point. Unknown birth time is supported.

02

Bring a real question.

Explore yourself, a decision, communication, pressure, love, leadership, a relationship, a family role, or a team challenge.

03

Add temporary context when it is useful.

Sovereign can use a short-lived snapshot of current astronomical conditions. It may make one topic more relevant, but it does not determine your behavior.

04

Include another person or group with permission.

Each person connects their own account and approves the information used. No one can grant permission for someone else.

05

Keep what helps. Correct what does not.

Save an insight when you want it available later. Unsaved conversations do not become a permanent personal record.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Your context does not disappear when the conversation ends.

Most AI starts from zero. Sovereign starts with the private personal foundation you created, then adds relationship, group, or temporary context only when it belongs to the question.

Inside Sovereign, this personal foundation is called your Baseline. It helps the product keep a consistent reference for communication, decision-making, connection, leadership, responsibility, and pressure.
