HOW SOVEREIGN WORKS

Your Baseline first. The situation second.

Sovereign starts with your private Baseline, then brings in the part of the situation that matters—what is happening now, another person’s shared context, or a wider system. You get the useful answer first and can inspect more detail when you want it.

ONE BASELINE · DIFFERENT SITUATIONS

Use the same foundation without treating every question the same.

A decision, a recurring pattern, a relationship, and a family or team can all need different context. Sovereign starts from the same Baseline, then uses only what belongs to that situation.

THE FOUNDATION

One private reference across the product.

Baseline Design gives Sovereign a consistent reference for patterns in decision-making, communication, connection, leadership, learning, pressure, and growth.
