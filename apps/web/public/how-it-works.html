HOW SOVEREIGN WORKS

Ask naturally. Get an answer from a foundation built around you.

Sovereign.OS is a private personal AI. It uses your Baseline as a starting point, then adds current, relationship, or system context only when you choose and the required permission exists.

FIVE CONNECTED STEPS

Build a reference point. Keep exploring.

Begin with yourself. Expand with permission. Each step preserves the difference between exact source data, interpretation, current context, and what only a person can confirm.

01

Build your Baseline

Add your birth date, birthplace, and birth time when known. Sovereign.OS calculates a private personal foundation it can use across future questions.

02

Ask the question you already have

Ask about yourself, a decision, pressure, communication, love, leadership, boundaries, Shadow, Gift, Alignment, or any part of your life you want to understand more clearly.

03

Add what is happening now

Current astronomical conditions can show which Baseline themes may be more relevant for a limited time. They add context; they do not determine your behavior.

04

Add another person or system when it matters

Invite another person to share only what they choose. Bring permitted Baselines together in a relationship, family, household, team, or group.

05

Confirm, correct, and keep what helps

Tell Sovereign what fits. Correct what does not. Save only the understanding you want available later.
