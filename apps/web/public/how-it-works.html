HOW IT WORKS

Build your Baseline once. Then ask about your real life.

Your Baseline gives Sovereign a consistent starting point. Ask about yourself or a decision. Add another person or a group only when the question includes them and the required permission exists.

FIVE SIMPLE STEPS

Sovereign shows the direct answer first and keeps the calculations underneath.

Facts, interpretation, temporary context, and what is still unknown remain visibly separate so you can understand and correct the answer.

01

Build your private Baseline.

Add the birth details used for the calculation. You can continue even when your birth time is unknown.

02

Ask a normal question.

Ask about yourself, a decision, communication, pressure, love, leadership, a relationship, a family role, or a team problem.

03

Add temporary current context when you choose.

Sovereign can use a short-lived snapshot of current astronomical conditions. This may make a Baseline topic more relevant, but it does not determine your behavior.

04

Add another person or group only with permission.

The other person connects their own account and chooses exactly what Sovereign may use. You cannot grant permission for them.

05

Confirm, correct, and save only what helps.

Tell Sovereign what fits and what does not. Save a useful insight when you want it available later—not every conversation.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Sovereign does not start from a blank chat.

Your Baseline gives it a consistent reference for how you may decide, communicate, learn, connect, lead, create, and respond under pressure.

Shadow describes how a useful quality may become narrow or overextended under pressure. Gift describes what that same quality can make possible when used with awareness. Alignment helps you compare a choice with what actually fits your needs, responsibilities, and tradeoffs.

Where the Baseline comes from

Baseline Design uses calculated astronomical positions and selected interpretive frameworks, including astrology, partial Human Design and Gene Keys activations, and numerology. These sources are used for reflection. They are not scientific personality measurements, diagnosis, or prediction.
